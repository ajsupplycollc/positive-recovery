Two Recoveries. One Mission.

PAWsitive Recovery, Inc. was founded on a simple but powerful idea: healing happens best when it's shared. We rescue animals from shelters, streets, and dangerous situations — and we pair them with residents of sober living facilities who are rebuilding their own lives.

"When someone in recovery cares for an animal that also needs saving, both lives are transformed. Responsibility becomes purpose. Compassion becomes healing."

Through our partnership with Jimmy Safe Place of South Florida, a licensed sober living facility, residents gain structured responsibility, emotional connection, and daily purpose by helping rehabilitate rescued animals. The animals receive the consistent care, patience, and love they need to heal and find forever homes.

What We Do

Programs That Transform Lives

Every program at PAWsitive Recovery, Inc. is designed around our dual-recovery model — creating outcomes for both rescued animals and the people who care for them.

🚨

Animal Rescue & Intake

We pull animals from overcrowded shelters, respond to emergency surrender situations, and rescue strays from dangerous conditions across Miami-Dade and Broward counties.

  • Emergency intake from kill-list shelters
  • Veterinary assessment and medical care
  • Behavioral evaluation and needs planning
  • Safe temporary housing and stabilization
🌟

Pet Rehabilitation

Many rescued animals arrive traumatized, undertrained, or with medical needs. Our rehabilitation program provides the time and care they need to become adoptable.

  • Structured behavioral rehabilitation
  • Socialization with people and other animals
  • Ongoing veterinary and nutritional support
  • Adoption readiness preparation
🤝

Sober Living Partnership

Through our partnership with Jimmy Safe Place of South Florida, residents in recovery provide daily care for rescued animals — building responsibility, routine, and emotional connection.

  • Daily animal care responsibilities for residents
  • Structured routine that supports sobriety
  • Emotional bonding and stress reduction
  • Job skills in animal care and handling
